Instructions

Tray 1 → ₱20. Tray 2 → run a magnet over it: coins that stick are new ₱10; of the rest, all-gold coins are old ₱5 and silver-ring coins are old ₱10. Tray 3 → ₱5. Tray 4 → old ₱1. Pan → new ₱1. Old ₱1 coins from 1995–2003 (no magnet pull) weigh 6.1 g; if you have many, weigh them separately.

Weighing coins inside their tray? Weigh each empty tray once and type it into that row's Container box; the page remembers it. To set all trays at once, type it in Container weight (all rows) at the top: it fills every row's Container box, and you can still change a single row after (set it to 0 if you press TARE on the scale).

Grams per coin calibration

Counts are about ±1%, because worn coins weigh a little less. For a closer count, calibrate: weigh 20 of your own coins of one kind, divide by 20, and type that into "Grams per coin". The page remembers your numbers on this device.
